# Beyond Wealth registration page

## What I’ll build
- A single polished white registration page titled “Beyond Wealth”.
- Fresh, crisp recreations of the supplied Incube Capital, Beyond Wealth, and 5 Years Completed artwork.
- A registration form for KYC name, mobile number, email, total members, adults, and optional children.
- One age dropdown per child, with choices from 1 through 18.
- Clear validation, mobile-friendly layout, and a success confirmation after a valid submission.

## Page structure
- Branded header featuring Incube Capital and the five-year milestone.
- Event identity area for Beyond Wealth.
- Focused registration area with accessible labels and concise supporting copy.
- Responsive layout that remains easy to complete on phones and desktops.

## Technical details
- Rebuild the uploaded references as cleaned image assets rather than displaying the blurry screenshots.
- Keep the form state in React and dynamically derive child-age selectors from the children count.
- Validate that adults plus children equals the total number of members, while keeping children optional.
- Add page-specific title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata.
